## Common Washing Machine Problems We Fix

- **Not draining water:** blocked drain pump, clogged filter or kinked hose.
- **Water leaking:** worn door seal, loose hoses or a damaged pump.
- **Not spinning:** faulty motor, worn belt or an unbalanced load sensor.
- **Not starting or power issues:** control board, door lock or wiring faults.
- **Loud noise or vibration:** worn bearings, shock absorbers or loose drum parts.
- **Door will not open or close:** broken door lock or latch.
- **Not filling with water:** blocked inlet valve or low water pressure.
- **Bad smell:** mold and detergent build-up inside the drum and seal.

## Repair or Replace?

Most washing machine faults are cheap to fix compared with buying a new machine. Repair usually makes sense when the machine is under about 8 years old and the fault is a pump, valve, belt, seal or lock. Replacement may be better when the drum or main motor is badly damaged on a very old machine. Our technician will give you honest advice either way.

## Simple Tips to Avoid Breakdowns

- Do not overload the machine.
- Clean the drain filter every 2 to 3 months.
- Leave the door open after washing to stop mold and smell.
- Use the right amount of detergent.
- Run a hot cleaning cycle once a month.
